# Automotive Journalism

Automotive journalism has been the central specialization of my 18+ year editorial career and remains both my professional expertise and personal passion.

## Areas Covered

- Automotive news and industry reporting
- New vehicle launches
- Full vehicle reviews
- Buying guides
- Luxury and performance cars
- Supercars and hypercars
- Classic cars and automotive heritage
- Motorsport
- Automotive technology
- Automotive events and exhibitions
- Automotive videos and multimedia
- Sponsored automotive content

## Regional Experience

My career has included work with leading MENA automotive media platforms, including **Top Gear Middle East, ArabGT, Arabsturbo, and Motorgy**.

I have covered major regional automotive events, produced interviews and launch coverage, written high-volume daily news, created reviews and test-drive content, and developed editorial programs focused on automotive history and enthusiast culture.

## Editorial Perspective

My interest in cars extends from the heritage and craftsmanship of classic vehicles to the performance, engineering, technology, and design of modern supercars and electric vehicles.

This combination of technical understanding, industry knowledge, and editorial experience allows me to translate complex automotive subjects into clear and engaging content for both enthusiasts and general audiences.
